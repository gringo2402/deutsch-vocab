🏫 Classroom

One grammar topic a week, at the level of Schritte plus Neu 5 (B1.1). Each topic explains the logic, the rules and the exceptions, with examples in German and their translations, and a short self-check. Swiss spelling (ss, never the Eszett).

Rules checked against grammis, the online grammar of the Leibniz Institute for the German Language (IDS Mannheim). Each topic lists the pages it used. Explanations, examples and translations are original.
