Deutsch vocab

Three word lists for the step from A2 to B1, built for work in business and technology. Works offline: open the files straight from this folder.

How the colours work

der masculine die feminine das neuter die plural (always neutral grey)

Every noun card has a coloured edge in its gender colour, so the article sticks visually. Level badges come from the Goethe-Institut lists:

A1A2B1 above B1 content word not in the A1–B1 lists not listed function word or adverb the lists don't show as a headword

How to study with a list

  • Filter by theme, level or word type, or search in German or English.
  • 🎯 Test me hides the English. Say the meaning, then tap the card to check. 🔀 Shuffle mixes the order.
  • 🔊 reads the word or the example aloud (Swiss German voice if your system has one).
  • ⬇ Export for Anki downloads the words you have filtered as a tab-separated file. In Anki: File › Import, pick the file, check that field 1 = Front and field 2 = Back.
  • Print a filtered list from the browser for paper review; English is always shown in print.
Sources: IDS Mannheim DeReWo, Leipzig Corpora Collection, Goethe-Institut word lists, Wiktionary-derived noun data. See _data/SOURCES.md.
